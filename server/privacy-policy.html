Our commitment to your privacy

Information We Collect

Personal Information

How We Use Your Information
Our data usage practices

Your information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Account creation and secure authentication
  • Processing service requests and transactions
  • Managing user tasks and reward distribution
  • Sending important notifications and updates
  • Verifying user identity through email verification
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing fraud
  • Improving our services and user experience
  • Administrative purposes and record-keeping

Important Commitment: We never sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely for providing and improving our services. We respect your privacy and maintain strict data handling practices.

Data Security
How we protect your information

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information:

  • Password encryption using bcrypt hashing with salt rounds
  • Secure JWT (JSON Web Token) authentication system
  • Encrypted data transmission via HTTPS protocol
  • Secure database storage with strict access controls
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Administrative action logging for accountability
  • Multi-layer security architecture

While we strive to protect your data with the highest security standards, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We continuously work to improve our security measures and stay ahead of emerging threats.

Data Storage & Retention
Where and how long we keep data

Your data is stored securely in our encrypted databases and may include:

  • User account information and profiles
  • Service requests and transaction history
  • Task submissions and earned rewards
  • Event participation records
  • Uploaded files and attachments
  • Communication history and preferences

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through your account settings or by contacting our support team.

Cookies & Tracking
How we use cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Maintain your login session across pages

Children's Privacy
Protection for minors

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have inadvertently collected information from your child, please contact us immediately. We will promptly investigate and delete such information from our systems.

We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with applicable laws such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
